Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Camera Calibrate Pro ("the app") turns an iPhone into a calibration target for a head-worn camera. This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not send any information to us or to anyone else, has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

  • Session log. During a calibration the app writes a file to its own storage on your phone: the phone's motion (gyroscope and accelerometer), the distance between the phone and your face, and which markers were shown when. The file leaves your phone only if you share it yourself with the share button.
  • Screen scale. If you do the ruler or bank-card check, the measured pixel size is saved on the phone for the next calibration.

Camera

The front camera is used through Apple's ARKit face tracking only to measure the phone's distance from your face while you calibrate. No image or video is stored or sent. Face tracking data is used while the app is open and is not kept, apart from the distance values written to the session log.

Motion

The motion sensors check that the phone was tilted during the tilt step and are written to the session log described above.

Deleting data

Delete a session log from the app's folder in the Files app, or delete the app to remove all of its data.

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no information from anyone.
